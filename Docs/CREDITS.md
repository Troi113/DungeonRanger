The Poly Pizza item models were re-oriented (upright, grip at the bottom), rescaled to unit size, and recentered for the game (`Tools/normalize_obj.py`); the Axe and Spear were converted from GLB to OBJ with flat colors (`Tools/glb_to_obj.py`; the Axe's palette texture was baked to one color per triangle). Shapes are otherwise unchanged.

From the Rat Pack, only the FBX and palette texture were imported; `rat.blend` and the preview images were left out.

The pack's promotional "DoNotForget.png" image was not imported. The folder "UI Main Menü Icons" was renamed to "UI Main Menu Icons".

## Trap model

The trap model is the project's own design (made with Gemini as a three.js page, `low_poly_medieval_dungeon_trap_3d_color_customizer.html`), rebuilt in code in `PrototypeTrapModel.cs`.

## Music

Original tracks generated by `Tools/generate_music.py` for this project (no third-party audio).

## Design reference

Gameplay and architecture are inspired by Watabou's Pixel Dungeon (GPL-3.0). See `Docs/PIXEL_DUNGEON_REFERENCE.md`.
